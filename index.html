Perfect Sweep — Free Basketball World Cup Fantasy Game

Perfect Sweep is a basketball fantasy game set at the FIBA World Cup. It is a free basketball draft game: roll a historic national squad, sign one star into your five, and chase an undefeated Cup. No account, no download, no paywall. This FIBA World Cup game online is a browser basketball game at perfectsweep.app.

How to play

Three beats: roll national squads, draft your dream five, then win the Cup undefeated.

  • Roll. Each roll draws a World Cup side from 1986–2023. Sign one player into an empty slot — PG, SG, SF, PF, or C — then roll again.
  • Draft. One signing per roll. Twice per run you can swap nation or year. Before tip-off, pick Run & Gun, Balanced, or Lockdown.
  • Sweep. Play eight games (group stage through the Final). Win the Final and you are World Champions. Go 8–0 for the Perfect Sweep, plus a bonus game against the 1992 Dream Team.

Who it’s for

For fans who want a basketball fantasy game without a season-long club, and anyone who wants a FIBA World Cup game online they can finish on a break. It is a free basketball draft game and a browser basketball game — phone or laptop. A Daily Challenge shares one draw each UTC day. FAQ

What is Perfect Sweep?

Perfect Sweep is a free basketball draft game and basketball fantasy game built around legendary FIBA World Cup national squads. You roll a nation and year, sign one star per position, and chase an undefeated World Cup run in the browser.

How do you play this FIBA World Cup game online?

Roll a national squad, draft one player into an empty slot (point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, or center), and repeat until your dream five is set. Pick a tactic, then simulate eight games. The mark is to win the Cup undefeated.

Is Perfect Sweep a free basketball draft game?

Yes. Perfect Sweep is free to play. There is no account, no download, and no paywall. Open the site and tip off.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Perfect Sweep is a browser basketball game. It runs on a phone or a computer at perfectsweep.app — no app store, no install.

What does a Perfect Sweep mean?

A Perfect Sweep is an 8–0 tournament: you win every game from the group stage through the Final. That unlocks a bonus game against the 1992 Dream Team. Win the Final with losses along the way and you are still World Champions.

Can I play a Daily Challenge?

Yes. Each UTC day shares one draw for everyone. Draft that day's five, post a nickname, and compare efficiency on the daily board.

Is this an official FIBA product?

No. Perfect Sweep is an independent fan-made basketball fantasy game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by FIBA.

How do I share a finished run?

When the tournament ends, open your Tournament Card and copy the link. Anyone with the URL can see your five, the path you took, and whether the Perfect Sweep held.
